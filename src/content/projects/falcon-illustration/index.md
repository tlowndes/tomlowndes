## A pet project

Not every piece in a portfolio comes with a client, a brief and a deadline. Some of them are just a thing you couldn't stop thinking about. This is one of those.

It's a detailed vector line illustration of the Millennium Falcon, drawn as a blueprint-style poster: a top-down view of the whole ship, with a side profile floating above it.

![Millennium Falcon blueprint illustration](/falcon-blueprint.jpg)

## Why a blueprint

The Falcon is a flying pile of mismatched panels, vents, pipes, dishes and cockpits, which is exactly what makes it fun to draw. A blueprint treatment suited it. Reduce everything to white line on a deep, dark background and the clutter turns into pattern. You stop seeing "a spaceship from a film" and start seeing a machine someone could theoretically build.

Drawing it as two views, top and side, is what makes it feel like a technical drawing and not just a picture. The little four-point stars scattered around the background are the only concession to being a poster.

## Line work

The whole piece is vector line art, which means it stays perfectly crisp at any size. The challenge with a subject like this is consistency: hundreds of panels, hatches and engine details all have to share one line weight and one logic, or the drawing falls apart into noise.

**Design:** a vector line illustration in two views, set as a poster.

**Status:** a personal pet project, not a commission.
