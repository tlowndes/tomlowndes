## Eliminating the Rattle

Standard keyrings are loud, scratch screens in pockets, and create continuous auditory static on walks. 

The **Orbitkey Key Organiser** locks domestic keys flat inside an elasticated stack:

- **Zero-Jingle Enclosure:** Keys rotate out smoothly on friction washers and tuck away flush.
- **Peripheral Anchor:** Serves as the mounting post for the **Resqme escape tool** and the **SanDisk rescue drive**.
- **Ergonomics:** Prevents keys from poking outward or gouging phone screens through trouser fabric.
