## System Architecture

Keys are universally the first casualty of an absent mind. Tying long, messy cords to your pockets invites friction, while leaving cables in bags ensures you never have them when battery panic strikes.

The car key module solves this by looping the ultra-short **VAFOTON 240W PD 3.1 Cable (21cm)** double on itself directly through the keyring.

- **Form Factor:** Bends in half to form a clean, durable fob loop.
- **Output:** Supports up to 240W Power Delivery (E-Marker chipped), handling high-draw laptops, tablets, or phones without bottlenecking.
- **Cognitive Purpose:** Eliminates the mental tax of untangling loose wires while keeping emergency power physically attached to the vehicle ignition.
